Help Center

How do I reset my password?

To reset your password, go to the login page and click on "Forgot Password". Enter your email address and follow the instructions sent to your inbox.

Why is my video buffering?

Buffering issues are usually related to your internet connection. Try these steps:
- Check your internet speed
- Restart your router
- Close other apps using bandwidth
- Lower video quality in settings

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your subscription at any time by going to your Account Settings > Subscription and clicking "Cancel Subscription". Your access will continue until the end of your current billing period.

Can I download content to watch offline?

Yes, with our Premium and Family plans, you can download movies and TV shows to watch offline. Look for the download icon next to the content you want to save.

How many devices can I use simultaneously?

The number of simultaneous streams depends on your plan:
- Basic: 1 device
- Premium: 4 devices
- Family: 6 devices
